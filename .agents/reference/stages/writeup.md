# The box's writeup

What `/wf-writeup` asks of the box besides what the skill says.

A decision is a position the project takes: which OS, which timebase, how the repository is laid
out. When working one out, ask what the hardware and its documents actually say. The decision says
which facts it rests on, and links the ledger for those still unverified. Positions that stand or
fall together may share one entry, each with its own `## ` section and its own `> [!TODO]`.
