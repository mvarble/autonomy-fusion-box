Everything here is unverified until the **Status** column says otherwise. A datasheet
can close a question about what a part is specified to do. Only a measurement can close
a question about what this hardware does.

Status values:

- **OPEN**: no usable answer yet.
- **DOC**: documented by the vendor, not yet measured on this hardware.
- **MEASURED**: confirmed on this hardware; link the write-up.

Source tags such as `[IM]` are defined in [Sources](#sources). Last reviewed 2026-10-30.
No item has been measured yet.

## Blocking M1

| 1   | Which F9R firmware is on this board?                                 | OPEN                                              | The key list and the UBX protocol depend on the version. Legacy `UBX-CFG-*` messages are unsupported from HPS 1.30, and UART2 UBX output needs HPS 1.30 or later `[IM §3.1.3]`.                                                                                                                                                                             | Poll `UBX-MON-VER` and record it in the M1 write-up. | 2   | Where are TIMEPULSE and EXTINT on this Fusion board?                 | OPEN                                              | The V3 guide lists labels `TPS` (3.3 V output) and `EXTINT` (3.6 V max) `[AS-V3]`. A search snippet of the Fusion product page says both are on a JST connector. The Fusion datasheet and product page could not be retrieved (403 or bot wall), so the position on this board's revision is unconfirmed. The V3 guide covers a different product revision. | Read the silkscreen, then probe the candidate pin with the AD3 **before wiring it to the Pi**. With a fix and the antenna outdoors, expect a 1 Hz, 100 ms pulse at about 3.3 V, in step with the PVT LED `[AS-BUDGET]`. There is no pulse before GNSS time lock, because `CFG-TP-LEN_TP1` defaults to 0 `[ID Table 101]`, so an indoor probe can look like a dead pin. |
| 3   | What is the TIMEPULSE swing and edge quality at each load?           | OPEN                                              | `Voh` is at least VCC − 0.4 V at 2 mA `[DS Table 9]`. Drive strength is selectable at 2, 4, 8 or 12 mA, default 4 mA `[ID Tables 55, 101]`.                                                                                                                                                                                                                 | AD3 analog channel at the far end of the wire with the Pi and Pico connected. Check for overshoot and rise time. Raise `CFG-TP-DRSTR_TP1` only if the edge needs it.                                                                                                                                                                                                   |
| 4   | Which OS, and how does ROS 2 reach it?                               | OPEN, position in `foundations`, not yet approved | Raspberry Pi OS Trixie with ROS 2 Jazzy from RoboStack, installed with Pixi. See [OS decision evidence](#os-decision-evidence).                                                                                                                                                                                                                             | Approve `foundations`, then close #29 on the Pi.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                       |
| 5   | What supplies coarse time to chrony after a reboot?                  | OPEN                                              | A PPS refclock needs another source for full time `[CHRONY]`. Only one process can own the F9R USB CDC port.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                | Proposed: a small host process owns the port, feeds a chrony `SOCK` refclock and forwards the bytes to the ROS node, so time sync works without ROS or a container. Close by power-cycling the box and timing PPS selection.                                                                                                                                           |
| 6   | How large are the Pi 5 PPS bias and jitter on this box?              | OPEN                                              | A third-party measurement puts `STAMP − IN` at about 11.7 µs with RP1 L1 enabled and about 6.2 µs with it disabled, mostly idle `[SATPULSE]`. Those are not our numbers.                                                                                                                                                                                    | M1 experiments B to D, idle and loaded.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                |
| 7   | What is the sign of chrony's refclock `offset`?                      | OPEN                                              | `[SATPULSE]` says `offset 11e-6` for a pulse 11 µs late. `[CHRONY]` does not state a sign convention for refclocks.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                         | M1 experiment C: apply the offset and confirm the error shrinks. A wrong sign doubles it.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                              |
| 8   | How accurate is the AD3 timebase?                                    | OPEN                                              | Not found in the specification sheet excerpts searched `[AD3]`.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                             | M1 experiment A: measure the PPS period against the AD3 clock.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                         |
| 9   | Do settings survive a power cycle?                                   | OPEN                                              | RAM settings are lost at power-down, BBR settings last while the backup supply remains, and flash is permanent `[IM §3.1]`. Whether this board has a backup supply is unknown.                                                                                                                                                                              | Set a non-default key in RAM and BBR only, power-cycle with the box off for a while, then `VALGET`. The config tool writes and reads back on every start regardless.                                                                                                                                                                                                   |
| 29  | Does the Pixi environment run on the Pi 5's default 16K-page kernel? | OPEN                                              | The default `kernel_2712.img` uses 16K pages `[RPI-CONFIG]`. Pixi's arm64 release builds were fixed for this by setting jemalloc's page size, and a build script on `main` still sets it `[PIXI-16K]`. On 2026-10-30, 221 ELF files in a sample of 17 packages had 64K segment alignment, which suggests they load, not that they run correctly.            | On the Pi: `pixi --version`, `pixi install`, a talker and listener, and a short MCAP recording. If something assumes 4K pages, `kernel=kernel8.img` is the remedy, and that is a boot-config write that needs approval first.                                                                                                                                          |

## Blocking M2

| #   | Question                                             | Status                 | What the docs say                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                    | How we close it                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                               |
| --- | ---------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| 10  | How does `ESF-RAW.sTtag` map to GNSS time?           | OPEN, **highest risk** | `[ID §3.11.4]` defines `sTtag` only as "sensor time tag", with no unit or epoch. `ESF-RAW` has no `iTOW`. `[IM §3.2.5.2]` gives a 1 ms time tag resolution and `CFG-SFCORE-SEN_TTAG_*`, but in the context of `ESF-MEAS`. `ESF-MEAS.calibTtag` is not usable: `calibTtagValid` is always zero `[ID §3.11.3]`. **The manuals do not give a mapping.** | (a) Tick rate: `sTtag` deltas against sample count, then long-run against `iTOW` over hours. (b) Wrap value: log through a wrap or read `SEN_TTAG_MAX`. (c) Offset: a shared physical event, such as a Pico-timed solenoid or tap seen in the accelerometer, with the AD3 on the actuator. (d) Ask u-blox support. Do not write a tick rate down from memory. |
| 11  | What is the IMU sample rate and message framing?     | OPEN                   | The rate is fixed and depends on the sensor. One sample per data type per message `[IM §3.2.7.6]`.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                   | Count messages per second and inspect the data types.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                         |
| 12  | How much group delay does the IMU have?              | OPEN                   | The part and its filter settings are not stated in the documents consulted.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                          | Part of the offset measured in #10(c). It does not appear in any time tag.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                    |
| 13  | Does the `sTtag` clock follow GNSS time or free-run? | OPEN                   | Not documented.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                      | Fit `sTtag` against `iTOW` and compare the slope with `NAV-CLOCK` drift.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                      |
| 14  | Do the logger and the Pi conflict on F9R ports?      | OPEN                   | UART1 is on the Arduino rail and UART2 goes to the XBee socket, on the boards ArduSimple documents `[AS-BUDGET]`. UART2 UBX output requires HPS 1.30 or later `[IM §3.1.3]`.                                                                                                                                                                         | Check which UART the logger uses and confirm its `RXM-RAWX` and `RXM-SFRBX` still arrive after our config runs.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                               |

## Blocking M3 and M4 (camera arrives Nov 16–29)

| #   | Question                                                                     | Status           | What the docs say                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                              | How we close it                                                                                                                                                                                                        |
| --- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ---------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| 15  | What is the trigger input voltage range?                                     | DOC              | J3 TRIG+ is 3.3–5.0 V `[INNO p.14]`.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                           | Confirm with the AD3. The opto has 200 Ω on board `[INNO p.15]`, so expect about 10 mA at 3.3 V using the vendor's 1.25 V `Vf`.                                                                                        |
| 16  | What is the trigger polarity?                                                | DOC, conflicting | Idle high, with exposure during the low pulse: exposure = low width + 14.26 µs `[INNO §4.1, SIGNAL]`. The 2023 manual says a rising edge triggers the frame `[INNO-2023 §3.4.2]` but that section is for the old Innomaker driver.                                                                                             | AD3 on TRIG+ and XTR. The schematic reads as non-inverting: LED off lets the 10 kΩ pull XTR low `[INNO p.15]`.                                                                                                         |
| 17  | How large and how asymmetric is the opto delay?                              | OPEN             | The schematic names PS2901-1 `[INNO p.15]`, the text says TLP281 on the strobe side `[INNO §2.4.1]`, and the board photo looks marked EL3H7 `[INNO p.14]`. Don't take timing from any one datasheet. Expected, but unmeasured: the falling XTR edge is the slow one, because it is phototransistor turn-off into a 10 kΩ load. | AD3 from TRIG+ to the XTR side, both edges, over temperature. Find the shortest low pulse that still produces a frame.                                                                                                 |
| 18  | Does trigger mode work under the chosen OS?                                  | OPEN             | Vendor support is Pi OS Bookworm and Trixie on Pi 5. Ubuntu is not mentioned `[INNO-GH]`. The Pi kernel has `imx296.trigger_mode` and a DT `trigger-mode` property `[IMX296-C]`.                                                                                                                                               | `modinfo imx296`, then run a triggered stream with a Pico.                                                                                                                                                             |
| 19  | Which way should trigger mode be enabled?                                    | DOC, conflicting | `stream_on` rewrites `0x300B` and `0x30AE` on every start `[IMX296-C]`, the same registers the vendor tool sets `[INNO-GH i2c.py]`. The vendor manual sets them after streaming starts `[INNO §4.6]`, and the vendor tool README says with the stream off.                                                                     | Use the kernel parameter. Read the registers back while streaming.                                                                                                                                                     |
| 20  | Does the strobe mark real exposure?                                          | OPEN             | The kernel driver does not enable it. It needs I2C writes with the stream off, and its start and end points are programmed registers `[INNO-V2 §6.2]`. It leaves through an opto with a 50 mA limit, so it needs a pull-up `[INNO §2.4.1]`. **So the strobe may not track exposure width.**                                    | Change the low width and see whether strobe width follows. Check that the writes survive `stream_on`. Treat the PPS-driven LED test as ground truth, not the strobe.                                                   |
| 21  | What happens when the Pico resets?                                           | OPEN             | A floating or low trigger line should hold XTR low through R5 (from the schematic).                                                                                                                                                                                                                                            | Reset the Pico mid-stream. Look for a bad long-exposure frame and a sequence mismatch.                                                                                                                                 |
| 22  | Does the libcamera timeout need extending?                                   | DOC              | Yes, set `camera_timeout_value_ms` in a PiSP config via `LIBCAMERA_RPI_CONFIG_FILE` `[INNO §4.4]`.                                                                                                                                                                                                                             | Test idle periods longer than the default, with the libcamera in the Pixi environment.                                                                                                                                 |
| 23  | Which I2C bus reaches the camera on each Pi 5 CSI port?                      | DOC              | I2C bus 4 is CAM1 and bus 6 is CAM0 `[INNO §4.5.2]`.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                           | `i2cdetect`; expect the sensor at `0x1a`.                                                                                                                                                                              |
| 30  | Does the conda-forge `rpi_fork` libcamera work with Trixie's kernel drivers? | OPEN             | The package contains the PiSP IPA module and `imx296_mono` tuning `[CF-LIBCAMERA]`. The solve gives 0.5.2 at RoboStack series 0.20 `[RS-JAZZY]`. The vendor's Pi OS support covers Pi OS's own libcamera, not this build `[INNO-GH]`. Whether the IPA module's signature verifies after Pixi relocates the package is unknown. | `cam -l` from the environment with the camera attached, then a triggered stream through `camera_ros`. If it fails, compare with the host's apt libcamera, then build `camera_ros` from source against libcamera 0.7.1. |

The official Raspberry Pi GS camera instructions do **not** apply to this board: 1.8 V
XTR, the divider, and the Q2/R11 modification are for a different circuit `[RPI-GS]`.

## OS decision evidence

Raspberry Pi OS is Tier 3 for Jazzy, and the ROS docs suggest either Ubuntu 24.04 directly
or Ubuntu containers on Pi OS `[ROS-PI]`. RoboStack is a third route, outside those tiers
`[ROBOSTACK]`. The argument is in the [foundations writeup](../../archive/foundations/writeup.md).

| Concern                      | Ubuntu 24.04 + native ROS 2                                                                                                                  | Pi OS + ROS 2 in Docker                                        | Pi OS + ROS 2 from Pixi                                                                                        |
| ---------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| ROS 2 support                | Tier 1 arm64                                                                                                                                 | Tier 1 inside the container, Tier 3 on the host                | Community-built `[ROBOSTACK]`, no ROS support tier                                                             |
| Innomaker trigger and strobe | Not mentioned by the vendor                                                                                                                  | Documented on Pi 5 for Bookworm and Trixie `[INNO-GH]`         | The same                                                                                                       |
| `imx296.trigger_mode`        | **Unverified** on the Ubuntu kernel                                                                                                          | Present in `rpi-6.12.y` `[IMX296-C]`                           | The same                                                                                                       |
| `pps-rp1` overlay with echo  | Needs Pi kernel `1:6.12.70-1+rpt1` or later `[SATPULSE]`. The noble listing suggests a 6.8 base `[UBU-PKG]`. **Unverified on a real image.** | Available at that kernel version or later                      | The same                                                                                                       |
| libcamera for Pi 5           | Build the `raspberrypi` fork from source in the workspace `[CAMERA-ROS]`                                                                     | Same build, inside the image                                   | Prebuilt `rpi_fork` `[CF-LIBCAMERA]`; see #30                                                                  |
| Device access                | Direct                                                                                                                                       | Pass through media, video, subdev, DMA heap and serial devices | Direct                                                                                                         |
| Time sync                    | Host                                                                                                                                         | Host                                                           | Host. Under every option the same host process owns the F9R port, so the coarse-time feed never depends on ROS |

On the Pi, whichever way ROS 2 is installed, check these on the real image first:

```sh
uname -r
getconf PAGESIZE
ls /boot/firmware/overlays/pps-rp1.dtbo /boot/firmware/overlays/pps-gpio.dtbo
modinfo imx296 | grep -i trigger
cat /sys/bus/pci/devices/0002:01:00.0/link/l1_aspm
```
