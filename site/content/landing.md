The box collects GNSS, IMU and camera data and records all of it on one timebase, disciplined by
GPS. Every measurement is stamped at its source with the time it was taken, never the time it
reached the computer. Each milestone ends in a write-up with numbers measured on this hardware,
and every fact that has not been measured yet is listed in the
[open questions](reference/open-questions/).

Decisions are worked through here. Each one is an entry in the [archive](archive/) with its
argument and its open questions, and it moves on only when it has been approved.
