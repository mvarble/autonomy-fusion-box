I trained as a mathematician and work as a navigation engineer, and I am not an embedded developer.
This site is the set of lab notebooks from my attempt to learn embedded work hands on, by building a real device with an AI assistant at my side.
The assistant helps me plan, explain and write up; the wiring, the bench work and the readings are mine.
Where I am new to something, the notebooks say so.

The box collects GNSS, IMU and camera data and records all of it on one timebase, disciplined by GPS.
Every measurement is stamped at its source with the time it was taken, never the time it reached the computer.
Each milestone ends in a write-up with numbers measured on this hardware, and every fact that has not been measured yet is listed in the [open questions](reference/open-questions/).

Decisions are worked through here.
Each one is an entry in the [archive](archive/) with its argument and its open questions, and it moves on only when it has been approved.
