A hands-on warm-up with the ArduSimple ZED-F9R board, before working through the ledger's open questions about it.
I am not an embedded developer, and I wanted a feel for the basics with my own hands first: find the documentation, configure UART1 in u-center 2 to send the IMU's raw samples, check that bytes flow through an FTDI adapter, then record them to a file and parse it, first through the adapter and then with an SD-card logger.
It answers no ledger question by itself; it sets up the probes that will.
